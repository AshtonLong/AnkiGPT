How it works

Source in. Deck out.

Most generators throw raw text at a model and hope. AnkiGPT builds an exam cheat sheet first, then writes cards from that — so every card is anchored to what actually matters.

01 / UPLOAD

Bring the material

A PDF (with an optional page range) or pasted notes. Text is extracted to clean Markdown so headings and structure survive.

Key concepts Formulas
02 / DISTILL

Build the cheat sheet

Each chunk is condensed into an exam-focused sheet using your focus, exclusions and must-include terms.

03 / DEAL

Generate & validate

Cards are written per section, checked for cloze syntax, math and scope, de-duplicated, then handed to you to edit and export.
